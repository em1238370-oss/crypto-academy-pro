# Project Overview — Check your crypto

## 🌐 Live Site
**URL:** https://crypto-academy-pro.onrender.com/

## 📋 What is This Project?

**Check your crypto** («Крипта без розовых очков») is a safety-focused crypto service: don’t trust gurus and ads — **check** channels, courses and exchangers before spending money.

- **Main page:** landing about checking crypto gurus and scam channels: Hero, live stats (LIVE), channel checker, top scam channels, “Check channel” and “Report scam” forms.
- **Sub-sites:** News, Risk (risk distribution), Basics (course), Coach — linked from the header.

## 🎯 Goals

- Provide a **channel checker** (database + Telegram: risk, reasons, losses, verdict).
- Show **live metrics:** scam channels per day, losses, fake-product courses.
- Collect **reports** via “Report scam” form.
- Inform via News and Risk sections.
