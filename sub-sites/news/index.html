Signal vs noise

You don’t need to chase every headline. You need a simple lens to separate real signals from the stream of noise.

  • Check the source Official regulators, exchanges, and audited research carry more weight than anonymous X threads or “insiders”.
  • Define the timeframe Is this a 1‑day story or a 1‑year story? A macro print or a meme tweet should not change your long‑term thesis the same way.
  • Link it to risk Ask: what does this change in liquidity, regulation, or fundamentals? If the answer is “almost nothing”, it’s noise.
