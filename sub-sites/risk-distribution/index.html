Rules That Keep You Safe

Discipline beats emotion. These rules protect your capital when markets turn against you.

  • Never invest more than you can afford to lose Crypto is volatile. Only use money you can lose completely without affecting your life.
  • Diversify, but don't over-diversify Spread risk across assets, but too many positions dilute focus and returns.
  • Set stop-losses before entering Decide your exit point before you buy. Emotion kills discipline in real-time.
  • Don't FOMO into pumps If everyone is talking about it, you're probably too late. Wait for pullbacks.
  • Take profits systematically Lock in gains at predetermined levels. Greed turns winners into losers.
  • Keep emergency funds separate Never risk money you might need for emergencies. Crypto can lock up for months.
  • Review and adjust regularly Markets change. Your risk tolerance changes. Update your strategy quarterly.

How Experts Protect Capital

Professional traders survive because they protect capital first. Here's how they do it.

  • Position sizing is everything Risk 1-2% per trade maximum. Even if you're wrong 10 times in a row, you survive to trade again.
  • Use the 2% rule Never risk more than 2% of total capital on a single trade. This keeps you in the game long-term.
  • Correlation matters Bitcoin and altcoins often move together. True diversification means different asset classes, not just different coins.
  • Protect with stablecoins Keep 20-30% in stablecoins. It's your safety net when markets crash and you need to wait.
  • Time horizon defines risk Money you need in 6 months shouldn't be in crypto. Match your timeline to volatility tolerance.
  • Track your risk-reward ratio Only take trades where potential profit is 2-3x your potential loss. Math works in your favor.
  • Cut losses fast, let winners run Exit losing positions quickly. Hold winning positions longer. Most traders do the opposite.
