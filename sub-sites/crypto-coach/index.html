🎯 Strategy Optimizer

Find optimal parameters for your strategy

💡 What this is: Describe your trading strategy using X and Y as variables. X = percentage price drop for buying (usually negative number, e.g., -10%), Y = percentage price rise for selling (usually positive number, e.g., +20%).

How it works: The optimizer will automatically substitute different X and Y values from the specified ranges and test each combination to find the best parameters for your strategy.

Example: "Buy when price drops X%, sell when it rises Y%" or "Buy BTC when it drops X% from current price, wait for recovery, then sell when price rises Y% from purchase price"
💡 X is the percentage price drop for buying (BUY trigger):
• Min (Minimum): The deepest price drop you want to test. For example, -25% means "buy when price dropped by 25%". The larger the number (in absolute value), the deeper the drop needed to enter.
• Max (Maximum): The smallest price drop to test. For example, -5% means "buy when price dropped by only 5%". The smaller the number (in absolute value), the earlier you enter the position.
• Step (Increment): The step between tests. For example, Step=5 means testing -25%, -20%, -15%, -10%, -5%. The optimizer will test all X combinations with Y values.
💡 Y is the percentage price rise for selling (SELL trigger):
• Min (Minimum): The smallest profit target to test. For example, 10% means "sell when price rose by 10%". The smaller the number, the faster you take profit, but it's smaller.
• Max (Maximum): The largest profit target to test. For example, 30% means "sell when price rose by 30%". The larger the number, the greater potential profit, but you need to wait longer.
• Step (Increment): The step between tests. For example, Step=5 means testing 10%, 15%, 20%, 25%, 30%. The optimizer will test all Y combinations with each X value.
